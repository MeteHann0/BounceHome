## 🇬🇧 About The Game (English)

**Bounce Home** is a 2D physics-based trajectory & puzzle game developed with **Godot Engine 4**.

Swallowed by an unexpected black hole, a little ball finds itself stranded in a distant, unknown corner of the cosmos. There is only one way back to its home universe and family: launch across **12 handcrafted worlds**, master each world's unique physics and hazards, and pass through every black hole in as few moves as possible!

### ✨ Key Features
- **12 Unique Worlds & Mechanics:**
  1. **Green Valley** – Classic trajectory & rebound puzzles
  2. **Sandy Desert** – Desert dunes and cactus hazards
  3. **Glacier Hills** – Slippery ice physics and low-friction surfaces
  4. **Mushroom Woods** – Bouncy mushroom trampolines & storm lightning
  5. **Volcano Island** – Molten lava pits and falling volcanic rocks
  6. **Cloud City** – Dynamic wind currents and flying birds
  7. **Crystal Cave** – Falling stalactites, bats, and crystal reflectors
  8. **Night Swamp** – Sinking swamp mud, floating logs, and fireflies
  9. **Lost Atlantis** – Low-gravity underwater physics, Poseidon cutscene, electric jellyfish & pufferfish
  10. **Cyber Neon City** – Swinging neon signs, moving reflectors & synchronized laser grids
  11. **Rusty Factory** – Rotating gears, sequential hydraulic presses, steam vents & conveyor belts
  12. **Final Horizon** – Multi-scale gravitational black holes leading to the final reunion
- **3-Star Rating System:** Earn up to 3 stars per level based on your move count.
- **Cinematic Story & Cutscenes:** Opening intro, mid-game Poseidon comic cutscene, and an emotional ending & credits sequence.
- **Bilingual Support:** Fully playable in **English (EN)** and **Turkish (TR)**.

## 🇹🇷 Oyun Hakkında (Türkçe)

**Bounce Home**, **Godot Engine 4** kullanılarak geliştirilmiş 2D fizik tabanlı bir nişan alma ve bulmaca oyunudur.

Talihsiz bir şekilde bir kara delik tarafından yutulan küçük bir top, kendini hiç tanımadığı uzak bir evrende bulur. Kendi evrenine ve ailesine kavuşmasının tek bir yolu vardır: **12 farklı dünyayı** aşmak, her dünyanın kendine özgü fizik kurallarını ve engellerini geçerek en az hamleyle kara deliklere ulaşmak!

## 📥 Download & Play / İndir ve Oyna (Windows)

> **🇬🇧 No Godot Engine required!** To play the game directly on Windows:
> 1. Go to the **[Releases (v1.0.0)](https://github.com/MeteHann0/BounceHome/releases/latest)** page.
> 2. Download **`BounceHome_Game.zip`** under the **Assets** section.
> 3. Extract the `.zip` archive into a folder (make sure `Bounce Home.exe` and `Bounce Home.pck` stay together in the same folder).
> 4. Double-click **`Bounce Home.exe`** and enjoy!

> **🇹🇷 Bilgisayarınızda Godot yüklü olmasına gerek yoktur!** Oyunu doğrudan indirip oynamak için:
> 1. Sağ taraftaki veya buradaki **[Releases (v1.0.0)](https://github.com/MeteHann0/BounceHome/releases/latest)** sayfasına gidin.
> 2. **Assets** altındaki **`BounceHome_Game.zip`** dosyasına tıklayarak indirin.
> 3. İndirdiğiniz `.zip` dosyasını bir klasöre çıkartın (`Bounce Home.exe` ve `Bounce Home.pck` dosyalarının aynı klasörde yan yana durduğundan emin olun).
> 4. **`Bounce Home.exe`** dosyasına çift tıklayarak oyuna başlayın!
